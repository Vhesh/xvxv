How the VNtradex Score works

VNtradex combines two simulated signals for each asset: momentum, the change in price over the current session, and sentiment, a score derived from related headlines in the News & Talks page. The two are blended into a score from 0–100. Scores above 75 lean toward "Buy" signals, scores below 25 lean toward "Sell" signals, and everything in between is treated as "Hold."

VNtradex Score, AI signals and predicted ranges are generated from simulated demo data for illustration only. They are not financial advice and should not be used to make real investment decisions. Always do your own research or speak with a licensed financial advisor before trading.
